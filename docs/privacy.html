Spanscreen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary

Spanscreen does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server run by us.

What stays on your devices

  • The screen picture and your touch and keyboard input travel directly between your Mac and your iPhone or iPad, over your local network, a peer-to-peer Wi-Fi link or a USB cable. They never pass through the internet or our servers.
  • Both apps store settings locally: the device name you choose, display preferences and the list of devices you allowed to connect.

Permissions

  • Local Network (iPhone, iPad and Mac): to find and connect to your other device.
  • Screen Recording (Mac): to send the picture of the Spanscreen display to your iPhone.
  • Accessibility (Mac): to turn taps and scrolls on your iPhone into clicks and scrolls on your Mac.

Network requests

The Mac app checks the public GitHub API once at launch to see whether a newer version is available. This request contains no personal data beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address, processed by GitHub).

Open source

You can read every line of code at github.com/codextde/spanscreen.
